## Common patterns noticed
1. Booking is the primary CTA, with the channel set by the market: online form (Japan), LINE (Japan/Thailand/Taiwan), WhatsApp (HK, Bali, UAE, Thailand), Zenoti/Fresha (UAE). Template should support a WhatsApp/LINE button as well as a form.
2. Japanese salons price by course (time + complexity, or fixed-price designs) and refresh seasonal/monthly design collections (Nail Quick, tricia, uka). Design galleries filter by colour, occasion, budget, artist.
3. Boutique studios keep the site minimal: hero, menu, gallery, booking, Instagram feed. Prices are often absent from the homepage (Stella, Ooh La La, Turquoise); the ones that show durations and inclusions (Nail Salon Tonic, Pixienailsbali) feel more trustworthy.
4. Trust blocks recur: Google rating and review counts, hygiene or sterilisation steps, certification (JNA), awards, repair guarantee.
5. Loyalty is light in boutiques (stamp card, referral, rebooking discount) and tiered in chains (Tips & Toes, POSH Perk Club, Diva Membership, point systems).
6. Colour library / lookbook / colour picker pages appear on Gulf chains (N.BAR, The Nail Spa, Sisters).
7. Pricing ladders by nail-art complexity ("levels 1-5", per-nail art) and "from" prices are the usual way to show price.
8. Salon + retail hybrids (Nail Artelier sets, Top Coat press-ons, ohora, uka) show a press-on/shop add-on is common; custom press-ons usually sell via Instagram DMs in SG/MY.
9. Hotel-spa nail offerings are sub-pages of the hotel site (Shangri-La LUXITA, Mandarin Oriental), usually with a request-an-appointment form and no catalog; a template would not copy these but could mimic the premium tone.
10. Themed branding is common (Nailaholics paradise, Daluna lunar, uka moon study, Ooh La La upscale-minimal).
