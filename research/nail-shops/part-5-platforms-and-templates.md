## Data model for a nail website generator

Derived from the features above (add-ons, deposits, packages, memberships, waivers, inspiration upload, allergies, two-tech services, travel for mobile).

- **Business**: name, tagline, brand tokens (colors, fonts, mood light/dark), locations[], phone, email, socials[], booking engine (native / Vagaro / Fresha / Square / other) + booking_url, currency, timezone.
- **Location**: address, geo, hours[day, open, close, breaks], holidays[], parking/accessibility, walk-in policy, waitlist.
- **ServiceCategory**: id, name (Manicure, Pedicure, Gel, Acrylic, Dip, Builder gel/BIAB, Gel-X/extensions, Nail art, Removal, Kids, Waxing), order, image, description.
- **Service**: id, category_id, name, description, duration_min, buffer_min, price (fixed | from | range), price_by_tech_level{junior, senior, master}, eligible_technician_ids[], requires_two_techs, deposit{type: percent|fixed|none, amount}, online_bookable, tags[] (vegan, hema-free, kids), popular, removal_question.
- **AddOn**: id, name (art per nail, chrome, french, repair, extra length, paraffin), price, extra_duration_min, applies_to_service_ids[], per_nail, max_qty.
- **Technician**: id, name, title, bio, specialties[], languages[], photo (real or placeholder), schedule[], service_ids[], price_level, instagram, accepting_new_clients.
- **GalleryItem**: id, image, alt, category (style, color, season), shape (almond, coffin, square), length, technician_id, service_id, tags[], linked_booking_preset ("book this look").
- **Package**: id, name, services[{service_id, qty}], price, validity_days, savings label.
- **Membership**: id, name, monthly_price, included credits/services, discounts, perks, billing, cancel and pause terms.
- **GiftCard**: denominations[], custom amount, delivery (email/print), expiry, redemption rules.
- **Loyalty**: type (points | visits | referral), earn rule, redeem rule, tiers[].
- **BookingRules**: lead_time_min, max_advance_days, slot_interval_min, max_services_per_booking, group booking (max party), same-day cutoff, deposit rules, cancellation_window_hours, late_cancel_fee, no_show_fee, reschedule limits, card_on_file_required, waitlist, reminders[], intake form (allergies, nail concerns, inspiration photo upload, medical/pregnancy, consent/waiver).
- **Policies**: cancellation, late arrival, redo/fix guarantee (days), removal, hygiene/sterilization statement, kids/guests, payment methods, tipping, accessibility.
- **Review**: id, author (first name + initial), rating, text, date, source (Google/Yelp/Facebook/Instagram), service_id, technician_id, verified. Only show aggregates that are real.
- **MobileService (optional)**: service_area (radius/zip list), travel_fee rules, minimum order, address capture, space/parking requirements, equipment provided.
- **Shop (optional)**: Product{name, brand, category (polish, press-on, care), variants (shade, size), price, stock}, retail attach at checkout, press-on size kit.
- **Page sections**: hero with Book CTA, service menu (tabs by category with price/duration), filterable gallery, team, packages/memberships, gift cards, reviews, FAQ/policies, location + hours + map, blog/care tips, sticky mobile Book bar.

## Cross-cutting takeaways

- Every platform treats add-ons, deposits and client preferences as separate objects; model them that way.
- Nail-specific differentiators: inspiration photo upload and two-tech services (Mangomint), allergies and design history (Vagaro, Fresha), shape/length filters (Apres).
- Membership and loyalty exist at salon level (Boulevard, GlossGenius, Mangomint) and brand level (Madam Glam $29 credit, Olive You Club, Apres Key).
- Vendor numbers (Fresha 25M, NailSoft 123M appointments, TGB 2B nails, Madam Glam 50,000 reviews) are claims; do not reuse as marketing figures.
