# Page anatomy

Generated by `scripts/make_catalog.py` from `templates/archetypes.json`. Do not edit by hand.

## Pick one archetype

Match the client's main offer, not its size. If two fit, pick the one whose first required section after the hero matches what the client sells hardest (cost savings, call quality, AI data, or breadth of services).

| Archetype | Use for | Dataset categories | Reference sites | Default preset |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| `enterprise-cx` | Multi-service CX or BPO companies with several delivery hubs, selling to enterprise buyers (banking, telco, healthcare, retail). | Global CX & Enterprise BPO, Vietnam & Southeast Asia Leaders | Teleperformance, Concentrix, Foundever, TDCX, Conduent | `enterprise-navy` |
| `offshore-staffing` | Staff augmentation and dedicated-team providers (Philippines, Vietnam, LATAM) whose main pitch is cost savings and fast ramp-up. | Philippines Offshore BPO & Staffing, Next-Gen Tech BPO & CX Startups, Finance, Accounting & Legal BPO, Nearshore & Global Software Outsourcing | MicroSourcing, Emapta, Sourcefit, SupportNinja, Wing Assistant | `human-warm` |
| `ai-data-ops` | Data annotation, RLHF, trust and safety, content moderation and data-heavy back-office operations. | AI Data Annotation & Operations BPO | Scale AI, Invisible Technologies, TaskUs, Toloka, Shaip | `dark-ai` |
| `cx-voice-sales` | Contact centers, virtual receptionists, telesales and SDR / lead generation teams where call quality is the proof point. | B2B Sales Development & Lead Gen BPO, Next-Gen Tech BPO & CX Startups | SquadStack, Smith.ai, Belkins, SalesRoads, CIENCE | `clean-minimal` |

## Slot order

Sections in page.json must appear in this order. Each slot is used once. Floating widgets (floating-chat, exit-intent-modal) go last.

- yes: must be present.
- recommended: include it when the client gave you the real content (logos, testimonials or case results, salary rates, call recordings). Leave it out otherwise; never fill it with invented content.
- no: optional; include it when it helps the pitch.
