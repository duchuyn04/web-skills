# Variant catalog

Generated by `python scripts/make_catalog.py`; do not edit by hand. The text comes from the first-line comment of each template.

## header (`templates/layout/headers/`)

- `bar`: Header 'bar': solid colour bar, category button with mega menu, wide search, hotline, account, cart.
- `centered`: Header 'centered': quiet announcement line, logo centred on top, category links in a row under it, search behind an icon, account and cart on the right (accessory boutiques). Hooks kept: [data-search], [data-search-input], [data-suggest], [data-cart-count], [data-account-label], [data-open="catdrawer"].
- `minimal`: Header 'minimal': white bar, logo left, main categories as text links, search panel opened from an icon, account and cart icons (Apple, Samsung stores).
- `rail`: Header 'rail': one slim bar with a Danh mục button, the logo, a wide search, account and cart. The button opens a wide slide-in panel that lists every category with its brands, price ranges and top products. Pick it when the shop has many categories and wants the page itself kept quiet.
- `split`: Header 'split': a dark thin row (announcement, stores, track order, hotline), then a white row with the logo, a wide search, account and cart, and a category row with a mega-menu button, the main categories as links and the hotline. Pick it for a general electronics shop that wants a calm white header.
- `tiered`: Header 'tiered': row 1 logo, wide search, hotline, account, cart; row 2 a category bar with icons (GearVN, Dien May Xanh). Colours come from the header tokens, so gaming (dark) and appliance (blue) both use it.

## hero (`templates/sections/hero/`)

- `bento`: Hero 'bento': one large tile and up to four smaller tiles in a grid, built from sec.slides then sec.side (GearVN home).
- `editorial`: Hero 'editorial': split layout, a large art panel on a tinted background beside a text column; 1-3 slides with prev and next. Uses the store.js carousel hooks: [data-carousel], [data-slide] (class is-active), [data-carousel-prev], [data-carousel-next].
- `showroom`: Hero 'showroom': a retail product banner per slide on the product's own colour (brand name printed large and faint behind the product, model name, tagline, four spec icons from the first spec groups, colour dots, price and button) with a tab strip of thumbnails and a progress bar under it; the product pictures follow the pointer and spread on hover. Pick it when every slide sells one product with real specs (Thegioididong, Sieu Thi Smartphone).
- `sidebar`: Hero 'sidebar': category list, carousel with tab labels, side banners (CellphoneS, Phong Vu).
- `split`: Hero 'split': a message column on the left (the first slide: label, title, text, price and button) and a 2x2 grid of product tiles on the right, taken from side banners first and then the other slides (4 tiles at most). Pick it when the home page should show four products at once next to one offer.
- `spotlight`: Hero 'spotlight': one product per screen, centered title, short line, price, two text links, large art underneath (Apple, Samsung).
- `ticker`: Hero 'ticker': a calm full-width banner (one slide, or a carousel with arrows when there are several) followed by a slim strip that scrolls product names with their prices. The strip pauses on hover and focus, has a pause button and stands still under reduced motion. Pick it for shops with many products and one main message. Data: slides, optional products (ids for the strip, default the first 12).
- `wide`: Hero 'wide': a full-width carousel of large banners, then up to four small promo tiles (Dien May Xanh, family appliance stores).

## card (`templates/partials/cards/`)

- `clean`: Card 'clean': large image on a soft tile, name, price from, colour dots, only the "Mới" label (Apple, Samsung stores). Keeps the store.js hooks of partials/cards/dense.html: article.pc[data-pc] with data-* attributes, [data-compare], [data-fav].
- `dense`: Card 'dense': Vietnamese mass retail card with discount, badge, spec chips, promo line, flash progress, rating, compare and wishlist. Every card variant defines card(p, flash, lazy) and keeps the hooks store.js needs: article.pc[data-pc] with data-id, data-price, data-brand, data-discount, data-rating, data-stock, data-released, data-facets, data-name; buttons [data-compare] and [data-fav].
- `gallery`: Card 'gallery': boutique card without border, tinted square image, the next colour shows on hover, name, ink price, colour dots. Keeps the store.js hooks of partials/cards/dense.html.
- `poster`: Card 'poster': a full-bleed square picture on a soft tile, the name and price laid over the bottom of the picture on a fade into the tile colour, discount and badge top left, compare and wishlist top right. Pick it for lookbook-like grids with few words. Keeps every store.js hook listed at the top of partials/cards/dense.html.
- `row`: Card 'row': a horizontal compact card with the picture on the left and, on the right, the name, one spec line, the price with the old price and the discount. Pick it for dense lists and for rails read on phones, where two rows of cards show more products at once. Keeps every store.js hook listed at the top of partials/cards/dense.html.
- `spec`: Card 'spec': gaming card that leads with the spec sheet: up to four spec lines, price large, discount and first badge (GearVN). Keeps the store.js hooks of partials/cards/dense.html.

## pdp (`templates/pdp/`)

Pick it for shops whose visitors mostly buy from a phone.
- `gallery`: Product page 'gallery': every colour image in a two-column grid on the left, sticky info on the right, then accordions (Mô tả, Thông số, Giao hàng và đổi trả), reviews and similar products (accessory boutiques). Hooks for store.js initPdp and qa.py: [data-pdp], [data-opt], [data-opt-btn], [data-opt-value], [data-v-price], [data-v-old], [data-v-off], [data-v-stock], .buybox [data-add-cart], [data-buy-now], [data-notify], [data-recent], [data-recent-track].
- `retail`: Product page 'retail': two columns, promo box, installment, store stock, add-ons, sticky buy bar (Thegioididong, CellphoneS).
- `showcase`: Product page 'showcase': large sticky gallery, a calm buy column, then the product told in feature blocks, full specs, reviews (Apple, Samsung stores). Hooks for store.js initPdp and qa.py: [data-pdp], [data-opt], [data-opt-btn], [data-opt-value], [data-v-price], [data-v-old], [data-v-off], [data-v-stock], .buybox [data-add-cart], [data-buy-now], [data-notify], [data-recent] with [data-recent-track], gallery hooks.
- `tabbed`: Product page 'tabbed': gallery and buy box side by side, then one sticky tab bar (Mô tả, Thông số, Đánh giá, Hỏi đáp) over four anchored sections; the bar also carries a small buy strip once the buy box scrolls away. Pick it for products with long specs and many reviews.

## plp (`templates/plp/`)

- `chips`: Listing 'chips': no sidebar; a row of filter chips that open small panels, sort on the right, roomy grid (Apple, Samsung, boutique stores). Keeps every hook of store.js initPlp: [data-plp], [data-filters] around all inputs, [data-price-min], [data-price-max], [data-filter-reset], [data-active-filters], [data-plp-count], [data-plp-count-n], [data-plp-empty], [data-plp-grid], [data-more], [data-more-n], [data-filter-count], [data-filters-open], [data-filters-close], [data-scrim], [data-sort-select]. Panels open with js/v/plp-chips.js.
- `rows`: Listing 'rows': products as wide rows (picture, name, spec lines, price, buttons) with a list and grid switch, filters as collapsible groups with counts in a side column. Pick it for categories where buyers compare specs, such as laptops, TVs and appliances.
- `sidebar`: Listing 'sidebar': filter column, brand chips, sort chips, Xem them (Vietnamese retail). Hooks for store.js initPlp: [data-plp], [data-filters], [data-plp-grid], [data-sort], [data-more].
- `tiles`: Listing 'tiles': a category banner with the intro on top, a column of links to the other categories, large two-column tiles, and filters inside a drawer that opens from a button at every screen width. Pick it for small catalogues where the picture sells the product.

## footer (`templates/layout/footers/`)

- `centered`: Footer 'centered': centered logo and tagline, one wrapped row of links, payment names, contact line and the legal line. Pick it for small shops that want a quiet footer.
- `full`: Footer 'full': promise row, five link columns, payment methods, legal line.
- `minimal`: Footer 'minimal': small grey link columns, contacts, payment names, legal and demo note (Apple, Samsung stores).
- `newsletter`: Footer 'newsletter': an email signup band on top (the address is kept in this browser and a toast confirms it), then four link columns and the legal line. Pick it for shops that send offers by email.

## cart (`templates/cart/`)

- `split`: Cart 'split': item rows on the left, sticky order summary with voucher and totals on the right, free-shipping bar, add-on rail.
- `stacked`: Cart 'stacked': one centred column with large item cards, then the summary card; on phones the total and the order button stay fixed at the bottom. Pick it for few, high-value items and mostly phone visitors.
- `table`: Cart 'table': a full-width table of items with a header row (product, quantity, line total), then a band with the voucher on the left and the totals and order button on the right. Pick it for shops where buyers add many lines, such as accessories or office equipment.

## checkout (`templates/checkout/`)

- `express`: Checkout 'express': one centred card with the fewest fields up front (name, phone, address), payment as large cards, the order summary folded at the top, and email, note and company invoice behind one link. Pick it for impulse buys and phone visitors.
- `onepage`: Checkout 'onepage': buyer, delivery and payment as numbered blocks on one page, order summary on the right. Fields live in form#co-form and stay visible at all times.
- `split-pay`: Checkout 'split-pay': one page in two columns. Left: buyer and delivery as open accordions inside the form. Right: a sticky card with the order lines, the payment choice, the voucher, the totals and the Dat hang button. Pick it when payment methods are the main decision (COD, transfer, installment).
- `steps`: Checkout 'steps': three numbered steps shown one at a time (buyer, delivery, payment) with a progress bar and Back and Continue buttons; the order summary stays on the right. All fields live in form#co-form; Continue carries data-step-next and checks only the visible step. Pick it for first-time buyers and phones.

## thanks (`templates/thanks/`)

- `compact`: Thanks 'compact': one small centered card with the order code, payment instructions and two buttons; items, totals and delivery fold into details. Pick it for shops with simple orders and a clean look.
- `receipt`: Thanks 'receipt': a green tick with the order code, items and totals on the left, payment and delivery details with tracking buttons on the right.
- `timeline`: Thanks 'timeline': a confirmation strip with the order code, then a vertical "what happens next" timeline built only from shop hours and shipping data, with the order summary beside it. Pick it when buyers ask what comes after paying.

## account (`templates/account/shells/`)

- `hub`: Account shell 'hub': a greeting card on top and a compact side menu; on phones the menu opens as a bottom sheet from a button in the card. Pick it for shops with few account pages and a calm look.
- `sidebar`: Account shell 'sidebar': user card and side menu on the left, the page content on the right.
- `tabs`: Account shell 'tabs': a user strip on top (avatar, name, phone, logout), horizontal tabs under it, the page content full width below. Pick it when account pages are wide tables or card grids.
