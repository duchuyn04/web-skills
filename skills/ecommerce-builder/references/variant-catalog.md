# Variant catalog

Generated by `python scripts/make_catalog.py`; do not edit by hand. The text comes from the first-line comment of each template.

## header (`templates/layout/headers/`)

- `bar`: Header 'bar': solid colour bar, category button with mega menu, wide search, hotline, account, cart.
- `centered`: Header 'centered': quiet announcement line, logo centred on top, category links in a row under it, search behind an icon, account and cart on the right (accessory boutiques). Hooks kept: [data-search], [data-search-input], [data-suggest], [data-cart-count], [data-account-label], [data-open="catdrawer"].
- `minimal`: Header 'minimal': white bar, logo left, main categories as text links, search panel opened from an icon, account and cart icons (Apple, Samsung stores).
- `tiered`: Header 'tiered': row 1 logo, wide search, hotline, account, cart; row 2 a category bar with icons (GearVN, Dien May Xanh). Colours come from the header tokens, so gaming (dark) and appliance (blue) both use it.

## hero (`templates/sections/hero/`)

- `bento`: Hero 'bento': one large tile and up to four smaller tiles in a grid, built from sec.slides then sec.side (GearVN home).
- `editorial`: Hero 'editorial': split layout, a large art panel on a tinted background beside a text column; 1-3 slides with prev and next. Uses the store.js carousel hooks: [data-carousel], [data-slide] (class is-active), [data-carousel-prev], [data-carousel-next].
- `sidebar`: Hero 'sidebar': category list, carousel with tab labels, side banners (CellphoneS, Phong Vu).
- `spotlight`: Hero 'spotlight': one product per screen, centered title, short line, price, two text links, large art underneath (Apple, Samsung).
- `wide`: Hero 'wide': a full-width carousel of large banners, then up to four small promo tiles (Dien May Xanh, family appliance stores).

## card (`templates/partials/cards/`)

- `clean`: Card 'clean': large image on a soft tile, name, price from, colour dots, only the "Mới" label (Apple, Samsung stores). Keeps the store.js hooks of partials/cards/dense.html: article.pc[data-pc] with data-* attributes, [data-compare], [data-fav].
- `dense`: Card 'dense': Vietnamese mass retail card with discount, badge, spec chips, promo line, flash progress, rating, compare and wishlist. Every card variant defines card(p, flash, lazy) and keeps the hooks store.js needs: article.pc[data-pc] with data-id, data-price, data-brand, data-discount, data-rating, data-stock, data-released, data-facets, data-name; buttons [data-compare] and [data-fav].
- `gallery`: Card 'gallery': boutique card without border, tinted square image, the next colour shows on hover, name, ink price, colour dots. Keeps the store.js hooks of partials/cards/dense.html.
- `spec`: Card 'spec': gaming card that leads with the spec sheet: up to four spec lines, price large, discount and first badge (GearVN). Keeps the store.js hooks of partials/cards/dense.html.

## pdp (`templates/pdp/`)

- `gallery`: Product page 'gallery': every colour image in a two-column grid on the left, sticky info on the right, then accordions (Mô tả, Thông số, Giao hàng và đổi trả), reviews and similar products (accessory boutiques). Hooks for store.js initPdp and qa.py: [data-pdp], [data-opt], [data-opt-btn], [data-opt-value], [data-v-price], [data-v-old], [data-v-off], [data-v-stock], .buybox [data-add-cart], [data-buy-now], [data-notify], [data-recent], [data-recent-track].
- `retail`: Product page 'retail': two columns, promo box, installment, store stock, add-ons, sticky buy bar (Thegioididong, CellphoneS).
- `showcase`: Product page 'showcase': large sticky gallery, a calm buy column, then the product told in feature blocks, full specs, reviews (Apple, Samsung stores). Hooks for store.js initPdp and qa.py: [data-pdp], [data-opt], [data-opt-btn], [data-opt-value], [data-v-price], [data-v-old], [data-v-off], [data-v-stock], .buybox [data-add-cart], [data-buy-now], [data-notify], [data-recent] with [data-recent-track], gallery hooks.

## plp (`templates/plp/`)

- `chips`: Listing 'chips': no sidebar; a row of filter chips that open small panels, sort on the right, roomy grid (Apple, Samsung, boutique stores). Keeps every hook of store.js initPlp: [data-plp], [data-filters] around all inputs, [data-price-min], [data-price-max], [data-filter-reset], [data-active-filters], [data-plp-count], [data-plp-count-n], [data-plp-empty], [data-plp-grid], [data-more], [data-more-n], [data-filter-count], [data-filters-open], [data-filters-close], [data-scrim], [data-sort-select]. Panels open with js/v/plp-chips.js.
- `sidebar`: Listing 'sidebar': filter column, brand chips, sort chips, Xem them (Vietnamese retail). Hooks for store.js initPlp: [data-plp], [data-filters], [data-plp-grid], [data-sort], [data-more].

## footer (`templates/layout/footers/`)

- `full`: Footer 'full': promise row, five link columns, payment methods, legal line.
- `minimal`: Footer 'minimal': small grey link columns, contacts, payment names, legal and demo note (Apple, Samsung stores).

## cart (`templates/cart/`)

- `split`: Cart 'split': item rows on the left, sticky order summary with voucher and totals on the right, free-shipping bar, add-on rail.

## checkout (`templates/checkout/`)

- `onepage`: Checkout 'onepage': buyer, delivery and payment as numbered blocks on one page, order summary on the right. Fields live in form#co-form and stay visible at all times.

## thanks (`templates/thanks/`)

- `receipt`: Thanks 'receipt': a green tick with the order code, items and totals on the left, payment and delivery details with tracking buttons on the right.

## account (`templates/account/shells/`)

- `sidebar`: Account shell 'sidebar': user card and side menu on the left, the page content on the right.

## Home sections (`templates/sections/`)

- `banners`: Banners: 2-4 colour tiles with a title, a line of text, a call to action and the product or device art. Pick it for current offers under the main banner.
- `benefits`: Benefits: a row of 3-4 promises with icons (delivery, returns, installment) taken from the shop's benefits list.
- `brands`: Brands: the shop's brands as plain name tiles linking to a brand search.
- `bundle`: Section 'bundle': a set of products sold together (PC build, streaming setup). One button adds every item to the cart. The cart charges each item's normal price; a bundle "price" is shown as the price staff confirm on the order.
- `categories`: Categories: one tile per category with the first product's picture and the product count.
- `collections`: Section 'collections': {"type": "collections", "title"?, "items": [2-4 of {"title", "text"?, "products": [3-6 ids], "bg"?}]} Each collection is a tinted panel with its title and text beside a short row of its products.
- `editorial`: Section 'editorial': {"type": "editorial", "title", "text": ["paragraph", ...], "product", "bg"?, "cta"?, "href"?, "align"?: "right"} a picture beside a short text.
- `flash-sale`: Flash sale: a coloured strip with an h:m:s countdown and a rail of discounted products with a sold bar. Use only with a real time-limited deal.
- `grid`: Grid: a product grid with a Xem them button, for a category or a hand-picked list.
- `hero`, variants:
  - `bento`: Hero 'bento': one large tile and up to four smaller tiles in a grid, built from sec.slides then sec.side (GearVN home).
  - `editorial`: Hero 'editorial': split layout, a large art panel on a tinted background beside a text column; 1-3 slides with prev and next. Uses the store.js carousel hooks: [data-carousel], [data-slide] (class is-active), [data-carousel-prev], [data-carousel-next].
  - `sidebar`: Hero 'sidebar': category list, carousel with tab labels, side banners (CellphoneS, Phong Vu).
  - `spotlight`: Hero 'spotlight': one product per screen, centered title, short line, price, two text links, large art underneath (Apple, Samsung).
  - `wide`: Hero 'wide': a full-width carousel of large banners, then up to four small promo tiles (Dien May Xanh, family appliance stores).
- `lineup`: Section 'lineup': 2-4 models side by side with the spec rows that tell them apart. Data: title?, products (2-4 ids), rows (spec row names as written in products[].specs, or facet keys).
- `needs`: Section 'needs': shop by need. Each tile opens a category listing already filtered through the URL (brand, price or a facet key).
- `newsletter`: Section 'newsletter': {"type": "newsletter", "title", "text"?} an email form. On a demo shop the address stays in this browser.
- `rail`: Rail: a horizontal row of products with brand filter chips and an all-products link, for one category.
- `rooms`: Section 'rooms': shop by room. Each tile links to a category listing, optionally pre-filtered (query keys are facet keys, brand or price). Data: {"type": "rooms", "title"?, "items": [{"title", "text"?, "category" or "href", "query"?: {"key": "value"}, "product"?, "bg"? (light colour)}]}
- `services`: Section 'services': the shop's service promises (delivery, installation, warranty at home). Every promise must come from the brief. Data: {"type": "services", "title"?, "items": [{"icon", "title", "text"}]}
- `spotlight-tiles`: Section 'spotlight-tiles': 2-4 large tiles in two columns, each a product on its own colour. Item: title, text?, product (its drawing or photo is shown), bg?, tone? ("dark" for white text on a dark bg), cta?, href?.
- `stores-cta`: Stores call to action: a band that says how many stores the shop has and where, with a link to the store locator.
