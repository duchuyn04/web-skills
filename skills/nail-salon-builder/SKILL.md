# Nail salon builder

You do not write HTML, CSS or JavaScript. You write one file, `salon.json`. `scripts/build.py` turns it into the whole site: home, price list, nail gallery, booking (service, branch, technician, day, time, contact), booking lookup and cancel, branches, technicians, offers, FAQ, policies, 404, and an admin. Templates, styles, scripts and the nail drawings already exist. Your job is to choose well and fill in the salon's real content.

`SKILL` below means the folder that holds this file. If you cannot run shell commands, stop and tell the user: the build is required.

## Which path

| The user | Path |
|:---|:---|
| asks to look at a salon site this skill built ("xem giúp", "review", sends its link or a screenshot) | Review. Run lint and QA with `--sweep` on its `dist`, open the screenshots, then send one table: each problem with its kind (Broken: fails for any salon; Off-system: differs from the rest of the site; Taste) and the fix in salon.json. Change nothing until the user picks rows |
| asks to change a salon already built (`sites/<slug>/salon.json` exists) | Edit. Change only what was asked in that salon.json, keep style and layout, rebuild, lint, QA, report what changed |
| gives a site this skill did not build | Say in one line that this skill builds from salon.json and does not edit other code; offer to build a new site from their content |
| anything else | Build a new site: Step 1 onwards |

## What the output is

A static site that runs from a folder or any static host. Bookings, status changes, notes and admin edits are kept in the browser's localStorage, so the flow works as a demo: book on the site, open `admin/`, confirm it, and the customer's lookup page shows "Đã xác nhận". It is not a live booking system: there is no server, no SMS or Zalo message, no shared calendar between devices. `references/going-live.md` lists what a real launch needs. Say this in the report.

## The admin

`dist/admin/` is built from the same salon.json; you configure nothing except the optional `staff` list. Password `demo` (changeable in Cài đặt, checked in the browser only). What it has:

- Tổng quan: period picker (Hôm nay, 7 ngày, 30 ngày, Tháng này, 90 ngày) with comparison to the previous period; tiles for doanh thu, lượt khách đã làm, giá trị trung bình, tỷ lệ lấp đầy, lịch hẹn, hủy và không đến; daily revenue chart with the previous period dashed; Cần xử lý (bookings waiting for confirmation, one-click Xác nhận); Lịch hôm nay with the next step button; booking sources; services by revenue; technicians; new versus returning customers; a weekday by hour heatmap.
- Lịch theo ngày: one column per technician at the chosen branch, blocks coloured by status, a red line at the current time, a side panel to change status or assign a technician, double-click an empty slot to add a booking.
- Danh sách lịch hẹn: status tabs with counts, search, date range, branch and technician filters, next-step buttons with undo, CSV export; appointment page with services, add-ons, discount, technician, payment method, customer note, internal note and history.
- Khách hàng: segments (new, regulars, not back in 60 days, frequent cancels), visits, spend, favourite technician, notes for allergies and preferences, history.
- Dịch vụ và giá: edit price, minutes and visibility for trying on the site (the booking summary uses them); real changes go into salon.json.
- Thợ: today's load, revenue, occupancy, commission at the rate set in Cài đặt. Báo cáo: by category, payment method, technician and day, CSV.
- Data: on a demo salon (`"demo": true`) the build generates about 400 days of appointments and customers with weekday and holiday rhythm, so every chart has values. On a real salon the history is empty and fills only with bookings made in that browser. Say which case applies.
- "Thêm lịch hẹn" in the top bar records walk-ins, phone and Zalo bookings.

## Rules that are never negotiable

1. Never edit files in `dist/`. Change salon.json and rebuild.
2. Facts come from the brief. Salon name, hotline, Zalo, addresses, hours, services, prices, durations, add-ons, art levels, packages, membership, gift cards, promotions, warranty and policies must be the owner's. A missing contact or address becomes a bracket placeholder (`"[Hotline]"`, `"[Địa chỉ]"`) and goes in the report.
3. The build checks facts against the brief. Every price in salon.json must appear in the brief (150k, 150.000, 150.000đ, 150 nghìn, 1tr2 and 1,5 triệu are all read). Figures and promises in texts ("bảo hành", "miễn phí", "thuần chay", "không HEMA", "tiệt trùng", "hấp", "dùng một lần", "an toàn cho bà bầu", "chính hãng", "tặng", "đặt cọc") must be in the brief too. When a price is "báo giá khi tư vấn", write `"price": null`. Only when the user asks you to invent a whole sample salon do you set `"sample_content": true`.
4. `"demo": true` marks a sample: the footer and admin say the data is a sample, pages carry `noindex`, the admin is filled with generated history. A Google rating, review count and quotes are allowed on a real salon only when the brief gives them (quotes word for word).
5. Copy follows `references/content-rules.md`. The build fails on emoji, arrows, em or en dashes, curly quotes, banned AI words and translated-from-English phrases.
6. Use only the keys in `references/salon-schema.md`. Unknown keys are build errors.
7. Pictures, in this order: the owner's photos (next to salon.json, `image` on gallery items, categories, the hero); library photos (`"image": "lib:<id>"`, list with `build.py --photos [interior|polish|tools|nails|pedicure]`) for the hero, categories and story; drawn nail tips (`art`) for anything else. Library photos are stock pictures with open licences: the build credits them on `nguon-anh.html`, and refuses them in the gallery of a real salon, because a gallery says "our work". Technician photos only when the owner sends them.

## Step 1: Collect the brief

Ask one short message for anything marked * that is missing. Do not stall on the rest.

- Salon name*, hotline*, Zalo, Facebook or Instagram, email; WhatsApp, KakaoTalk or LINE if they serve visitors
- Branches*: address, opening hours per weekday, last order time, phone, parking
- Price list*: every service with price and time in minutes (a photo of the menu board is fine; if minutes are missing, ask, because booking slots are planned from them), add-ons (French, vẽ theo ngón, đính đá, tháo móng cũ), nail art levels
- Technicians: name, title, what they are good at, branch, day off
- Booking: on the website, through a platform link (Fresha, Booksy, Square), or by Zalo or Messenger only; cancel window; deposit
- Offers: packages (thẻ lần), membership, gift cards, promo codes; warranty, free removal and surcharges as the owner states them (they go under the price list); English names and dollar prices if their menu has them
- Rating and review count on Google or Facebook, quotes if they want them shown
- Logo colour or a colour they like; photos of their work and the room

If the user only wants a sample, use `examples/demo-nail.salon.json` as the model with `"demo": true` and `"sample_content": true`, invent a salon with a fictional name and say so.

## Step 2: Choose a style

| Style | Pick it for | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `warm-luxury` | boutique and spa-style salons, cream and taupe, serif headings, centered header | `examples/demo-nail.salon.json` |
| `k-minimal` | Korean and Japanese style studios and chains: white, soft pink, gallery up front | `examples/styles/k-minimal.salon.json` |
| `dark-glam` | nail art studios, late opening, glam: near-black, raspberry accent, full-bleed hero | `examples/styles/dark-glam.salon.json` |
| `playful` | colourful nail bars for students and young customers: bright, rounded type, collage hero | `examples/styles/playful.salon.json` |

Set `"style"`. Override parts with `"layout": {"header": "classic|centered", "hero": "split|stack|full|collage", "card": "line|soft"}` or per hero section with `"variant"`. Put the logo colour in `theme.accent`. Keep the style's fonts unless the brief names one; any other font must come from `data/fonts.json` (checked for Vietnamese marks, see `references/fonts.md`). Korean, Japanese, Chinese or Thai words in a bilingual menu need nothing special: the build gives them their own fonts. `python SKILL/scripts/build.py --list` prints styles, sections, fonts, nail art options and icons.

## Step 3: Start from the example

```
mkdir sites/<salon-slug>
copy the example of the chosen style  to  sites/<salon-slug>/salon.json
```

Then replace every value with the client's content. Put the brief, as given, in `"brief"`. Never keep the example's services, prices, technicians, reviews, packages or policies. `categories` group the menu; `services` carry `minutes` and `price`; `addons` lists what may be added to which service (`services[].addons`); `gallery` items take a photo (`image`) or a drawn look (`art`: shape, length, style, colours); `home` lists the home sections in order (start from the style's example; leave out a section whose data the salon does not have).

## Step 4: Build until clean

```
python SKILL/scripts/build.py sites/<slug>/salon.json --out sites/<slug>/dist
```

Every error names the JSON path and the fix. Run again until it prints `OK built`. When an error says a price or figure is not in the brief, delete the detail or ask the client; never add it to the brief to pass. Read the WARN lines (placeholders, empty categories).

## Step 5: Lint

```
python SKILL/scripts/lint_output.py sites/<slug>/dist
```

It must print `LINT PASSED`.

## Step 6: Browser QA

```
python SKILL/scripts/qa.py sites/<slug>/dist --sweep
```

(On Windows, if `python` cannot import Playwright, `py -0` lists versions; use one that has it, such as `py -3.11`.) It opens every page at 1440 and 390 px (script errors, sideways scroll, broken images, wrapped labels, small tap targets, hover that moves layout), narrows the main pages from 1440 to 360 px, then runs the flow: book a service with an add-on, stop an empty contact step, confirm, find it in lookup as "Chờ xác nhận", confirm it in the admin, see "Đã xác nhận", cancel online, add a walk-in on the day calendar, change a price in the admin and see it in the booking summary. It must print `QA PASSED`. Then open `dist/qa/index-1440.png`, `index-390.png`, `dat-lich-390.png` and `admin_calendar-1440.png` and look at them.

## Step 7: Report

Tell the user briefly, in their language:

- first, every choice you made for them, one line each with the reason and an offer to change it: style, layout overrides, accent, demo or real data, `sample_content`, booking mode
- where the site is (`sites/<slug>/dist/index.html`) and the admin (`dist/admin/login.html`, password `demo`); on a real salon say the admin is an unsecured preview until `references/going-live.md` is done
- what the admin shows and whether its history is sample data or empty
- how to view it: open the file, or run `python -m http.server` inside `dist`
- every placeholder and warning left open, durations you had to ask about, and the going-live items the salon needs before taking real bookings

## Reference files

- `references/salon-schema.md`: every key of salon.json with limits, sections, nail art options
- `references/content-rules.md`: how Vietnamese nail salons write, demo versus real data, what the build checks
- `references/patterns.md`: what 134 real salons do (section order, menu, price display, booking channels, styles), with the sites to open

## Adding a rule

At most five new rules per change, each with the case it applies to; say which rule it replaces; one rule lives in one file; a rule that catches nothing in three rounds of building comes out; after adding one, check the templates and examples against it. Write the rule and its reason, not its history.

## Maintaining the library

Templates are Jinja2 in `templates/`: `layout/base.html` (header, drawer, footer, mobile bar), `pages/`, `sections/` (home sections), `partials/` (macros, price tabs, offers, branch cards), `admin/`. Behaviour: `assets/js/common.js` (storage, appointment model, seed decoding, free slots, prices), `site.js`, `booking.js` (booking and lookup), `admin.js` (one function per admin page). Styles: `site.css` (all four styles; tokens come from `data/presets.json`) and `admin.css`, both flat (`references/flat-style.md`): the soft card style is a surface tone, never a shadow. `python tools/check_flat_css.py` in the plugin repository checks them (`scripts/flat_css.py` and `references/flat-style.md` are the same in every skill; change all copies together). Drawings: `scripts/nailart.py`. Demo history: `seed_data` in `build.py` (format in its docstring); seed days are relative to the build date and `common.js` moves them forward by whole weeks so today always has bookings. After any change rebuild the four examples, run lint and QA with `--sweep` on each, and look at the screenshots.
